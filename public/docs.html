SETU User Guide

Everything you need to manage companies, contacts, and sales opportunities in SETU — written for the sales team. No technical knowledge required.

🏠About SETU

SETU is Bluecom's internal sales CRM. Use it to track the companies you work with, the contacts at those companies, and the deals you are pursuing — all in one place, visible to the right people on your team.

  • Access: SETU runs in any web browser — no software to install. Open the link and sign in.
  • Login: Use your Microsoft (Bluecom) account. You will be taken to the Opportunities page after signing in.
  • What you see: The records visible to you depend on your assigned role. The next section explains what each role can access.
  • Opportunity IDs: Every opportunity has a unique reference code starting with setu- (e.g. setu-abc123). Use it when discussing deals with teammates to avoid any confusion.

👤Understanding Your Role

SETU has six roles. Your role controls which opportunities, companies, and contacts you can see, and what actions you are allowed to take. If you are not sure which role you have, ask your Admin.

Admin
Administrator
Full system access
  • Sees all opportunities, companies, and contacts from every user
  • Can delete companies, contacts, and opportunities
  • Has access to the Admin panel for managing users and territories
  • Can do everything other roles can do
TM
Territory Manager
Territory-scoped visibility
  • Sees all opportunities within their assigned territory
  • Can create, edit, and manage their own companies and contacts
  • Pipeline shows only their own created opportunities
  • Cannot delete companies, contacts, or opportunities
ISR
Inside Sales Representative
Own opportunities only
  • Sees only opportunities they personally created
  • Also sees opportunities where they are added as a POC (shared with them)
  • Can create and manage their own companies and contacts
  • Cannot see other users' opportunities
  • Cannot delete anything
AM
Account Manager
Own opportunities only
  • Sees only opportunities they personally created
  • Also sees opportunities where they are added as a POC (shared with them)
  • Can create and manage their own companies and contacts
  • Cannot see other users' opportunities
  • Cannot delete anything
BU Head
Business Unit Head
Single BU — all territories
  • Sees all opportunities that contain line items belonging to their Business Unit
  • Visibility spans all territories — filtered by BU, not geography
  • Can review deal composition for their BU across the whole business
  • Cannot delete anything
BU Mgr
BU Manager
Multiple BUs — all territories
  • Sees all opportunities containing line items from any of their assigned Business Units
  • Can manage visibility across multiple BUs at once
  • Visibility spans all territories — filtered by BU, not geography
  • Cannot delete anything

🏢Companies

Company records are the foundation of SETU. You must create a company before you can create any opportunities against it.

Companies page

Understanding the two tabs

The Companies page is split into two tabs, each showing a different set of records.

My Companies
  • Companies you personally created
  • Full details are visible, including pipeline value
  • You can edit these records
All Companies
  • Every company in the system, regardless of who created it
  • Basic info only: name, industry, type, and city
  • Pipeline value is not shown for other people's companies
ℹ️
Admins
Admins see all companies with full details in both tabs.

Adding a company

Add a company whenever you start working with a new client or prospect. Once saved, you can link opportunities to it.

  1. Go to Companies in the left sidebar — or click here to open it.
  2. Click Add Company in the top right corner.
  3. Fill in the required fields:

    • Company NameThe full legal or trading nameRequired
    • IndustryThe sector the company operates inRequired
    • Company Typee.g. Private, Government, SMERequired
    • CityWhere the company is basedRequired
  4. Add any optional details such as website or description.
  5. Click Save. The company appears under My Companies.

Bulk uploading companies (CSV)

If you have a list of companies ready in a spreadsheet, you can import them all at once instead of entering them one by one.

  1. Go to Companies — open it here.
  2. Click Bulk Import in the top right corner (next to Add Company).
  3. Click Download Template to get a CSV file with the correct column headers already set up.
  4. Open the template in Excel or Google Sheets and fill in your company data row by row. Do not rename, remove, or reorder any of the column headers.
  5. Save the file as .csv. In Excel: File → Save As → CSV (Comma delimited).
  6. Back in SETU, click Upload File and select your saved file.
  7. Review any errors — rows with missing required fields will be flagged. Fix them in your spreadsheet and re-upload if needed.
  8. Click Confirm to complete the import.
💡
Required columns
The template's required columns match the required fields when adding manually: Company Name, Industry, Company Type, and City. Every row must have all four, or that row will fail during import.

👥Contacts

Contacts are the individual people at a company — the decision-makers and points of contact for a deal. Each contact is linked to a specific company record.

Contacts page

Adding a contact to a company

Contacts are added from inside the company record — not from the Contacts page directly.

  1. Go to Companies and open the company you want to add the contact to.
  2. Click Add Contact inside the company page.
  3. Fill in the contact's name, email address, phone number, and designation.
  4. Click Save. The contact is now linked to that company and available to select when creating opportunities.

Who can see contacts?

  • You can only see contacts belonging to companies you created. This protects the contact information of other users' clients.
  • Admins can see all contacts across every company in the system.

💼What is an Opportunity?

An opportunity represents a potential deal with a company. Every deal you are working on should be logged as an opportunity so your team has an accurate view of the pipeline.

Opportunities page

Key information on every opportunity

  • Unique ID: Every opportunity gets a reference code starting with setu- (e.g. setu-abc123). It is shown in the header when you open the opportunity. Use it when discussing a deal with teammates to avoid any ambiguity.
  • Status — Draft: The opportunity is incomplete. Only you (and Admins) can see it. Nothing is visible to the rest of the team.
  • Status — Created: The opportunity is published and visible to your teammates based on their role and territory.
  • Stage: Where the deal currently sits in the sales process — see the flow below.
  • Value: The estimated deal size in INR. Update this whenever the scope changes.
  • Created date: The date the opportunity was created, visible in the details panel when you open it.

Deal Stages

Prospecting → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won / Lost
ℹ️
Won and Lost opportunities move off the Pipeline automatically and appear in the Concluded page instead.

➕Creating an Opportunity

Before creating an opportunity, make sure the company already exists in SETU. If not, add the company first — you cannot link an opportunity to a company that hasn't been created yet.

New Opportunity
  1. Go to Opportunities and click New Opportunity in the top right — or go to Pipeline and click New Opportunity there.
  2. Fill in the required fields:

    • TitleA short, descriptive name for the dealRequired
    • CompanySearch and select the company this deal is withRequired
    • Primary ContactThe key person at the company — select from that company's contactsRequired
    • TerritoryThe territory this deal belongs toRequired
    • StageStarting stage — usually ProspectingRequired
    • PriorityLow, Medium, or HighRequired
    • Close DateThe expected date this deal will closeRequired
    • ValueEstimated deal size in INRRequired
  3. Add Line Items — the individual products or services in the deal. See Line Items below for full details.
  4. Add Service Add-ons if applicable to the deal.
  5. Click Save as Draft to save your progress privately, or click Publish to make it live and visible to your team.

Saving as a draft

Use a draft when you do not have all the information ready yet. Your progress is saved privately — no one else can see it until you publish.

  • Click Save as Draft at any point while filling in the form.
  • Drafts appear on the Pipeline board with a Draft label on the card.
  • Click the draft card in Pipeline to reopen and continue editing at any time.
  • Drafts are visible only to you and to Admins. Other team members cannot see them until you publish.

Publishing an opportunity

Publishing makes the opportunity active and visible to your team. Do this when the deal is ready to be tracked by others.

  • Click Publish (or Create Opportunity) when the form is complete.
  • The opportunity status changes from Draft to Created.
  • It becomes visible to other users based on their role — for example, the Territory Manager for that territory will be able to see it.

📦Line Items & Business Units

Line items are the individual products and services that make up a deal. Each one is linked to a Business Unit — the internal team responsible for delivering that part. BU Heads and BU Managers use line items to gain visibility into deals relevant to their area.

The five Business Units

Select the Business Unit that matches what you are selling in each line item.

Business Unit Name BU Type Who sees this?
Business Continuity & Software SSG BU Head / Manager assigned to SSG
Cloud Business Cloud BU Head / Manager assigned to Cloud
Cybersecurity ISS BU Head / Manager assigned to ISS
Infrastructure Services Group ISG BU Head / Manager assigned to ISG
Networking Networking & AV BU Head / Manager assigned to Networking & AV
ℹ️
Why this matters
If you add a line item from the Cybersecurity BU to your opportunity, the Cybersecurity BU Head will automatically be able to see that opportunity — even if they are in a different territory. This is how cross-functional visibility works in SETU.

Adding a line item

You add line items while filling in the opportunity form. You can add as many as needed, from different Business Units, in the same opportunity.

  1. Inside the opportunity form, scroll to the Line Items section.
  2. Click Add Line Item.
  3. Select the Business Unit (BU) from the dropdown that matches what you are selling.
  4. Select the BU Owner — the person within that BU responsible for this part of the deal.
  5. Fill in the details:

    • DescriptionWhat the product or service is
    • QuantityNumber of units
    • Unit PricePrice per unit in INR — the total is calculated automatically
    • License CountNumber of licences — applies to Business Continuity & Software only
  6. To add another line item from a different BU, click Add Line Item again and repeat.
💡
Multi-BU deals
A single opportunity can include line items from multiple Business Units. For example, a deal might include both Cloud Business and Cybersecurity components. Each BU's Head will independently gain visibility into the opportunity once you add their BU as a line item.

🔗Sharing an Opportunity

Sometimes a colleague needs access to a deal that is outside their territory or role scope. You can share a specific opportunity with them by adding them as a Point of Contact (POC). This gives them visibility of just that one deal — nothing else changes.

  1. Open the opportunity by clicking on it from the Opportunities list or the Pipeline board.
  2. In the drawer that opens on the right, look for the Share button at the top of the drawer header.
  3. Search for the team member you want to share with and select their name.
  4. They are now added as a POC and can see this specific opportunity, regardless of their territory or role.
ℹ️
POC visibility
Being added as a POC gives access to one specific opportunity only. It does not expand the person's general access to other opportunities in that territory or BU.

📊Pipeline Board

The Pipeline page gives you a visual snapshot of all your active deals. Opportunities are shown as cards on a Kanban board, sorted into columns by stage — so you can see at a glance where every deal stands and what needs attention.

Pipeline page

What you see on the Pipeline

  • The board shows your own created opportunities — both drafts and published. Won and Lost deals are not shown here; they move to the Concluded page.
  • Each column header shows the stage name, the number of deals in that stage, and the total pipeline value for that column.
  • Cards with a Draft label are unpublished — click them to continue editing.
Prospecting → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation

Reordering cards within a stage

You can drag and drop cards within a column to prioritise deals within the same stage.

  1. Click and hold a card on the Pipeline board.
  2. Drag it up or down within the same column.
  3. Release to drop it in the new position. The order is saved automatically.
ℹ️
Drag and drop only reorders cards within the same stage column. To move a deal to a different stage, use the opportunity drawer — see below.

Changing the stage of a deal

To move a deal from one stage to another, open it and change the stage in the details panel.

  1. Click a card on the Pipeline board — or click an opportunity in the Opportunities list.
  2. The opportunity drawer opens on the right side of the screen.
  3. Find the Stage dropdown in the details panel.
  4. Select the new stage.
  5. The card moves to the correct column on the board automatically. No need to save manually.

🏆Marking an Opportunity as Won or Lost

When a deal reaches its conclusion — whether it was won or lost — mark it accordingly. SETU will ask you to fill in a short closing form before the opportunity moves off the Pipeline and into the Concluded archive.

⚠️
Important — deals are locked after closing
Once you mark an opportunity as Won or Lost and save the closing details, the record cannot be edited. Review everything carefully before confirming.
🏆  Mark as Won

Open the opportunity drawer, change the Stage to Won. A form will appear asking for:

  • Closing Remarks — a brief summary of how the deal was closed
  • Final Deal Value — the actual signed value (may differ from the original estimate)
  • PO Number — the purchase order number from the client
  • Expected Delivery Date
  • Key Decision Maker — the name of the person who approved the deal
  • Subscription Start / End Date (if applicable)
✗  Mark as Lost

Open the opportunity drawer, change the Stage to Lost. A form will appear asking for:

  • Loss Reason — why the deal was not won
  • Lost To — the competitor or alternative that won the deal instead
  • Could Be Revived? — Yes or No — helps identify future opportunities

After saving, the deal disappears from your Pipeline and can be found in the Concluded page.

✅Concluded Page

The Concluded page is the archive of all closed deals. Opportunities marked as Won or Lost live here. Records are view-only — they cannot be edited once concluded.

Concluded page
Won tab
  • All deals that were won
  • Shows final deal value, PO number, expected delivery date, and key decision maker
  • Click any row to open the full deal details
Lost tab
  • All deals that were not won
  • Shows loss reason, competitor/alternative, and whether the deal could be revived
  • Click any row to open the full deal details

Searching and filtering

  • Search: The search bar covers opportunity title, company name, opportunity ID (setu-xxx), territory, owner name, loss reason, competitor name, PO number, and key decision maker — search across any of these at once.
  • Date range: Use the calendar date inputs to filter by the date the opportunity was created. Or click a quarter button — Q1, Q2, Q3, or Q4 — based on the April–March Indian fiscal year to jump to that quarter instantly.

🔍Filters & Search

The Opportunities page has several filters to help you find what you need quickly without scrolling through the full list.

Opportunities page
Filter How it works

🗑️Deleting Records

Only Admins can delete records in SETU. Deletion is permanent — there is no undo once confirmed.

🔐
Admin Only
All other roles — Territory Manager, ISR, Account Manager, BU Head, and BU Manager — cannot delete companies, contacts, or opportunities. If you need a record removed, contact your Admin.

Deleting an opportunity

  1. Open the opportunity from the Opportunities list or the Pipeline board.
  2. In the drawer that opens on the right, click the Delete (trash icon) button in the top right corner of the drawer.
  3. A confirmation dialog will appear — read it carefully before proceeding.
  4. Click Confirm Delete to permanently remove the opportunity.
⚠️
Warning
Deleting an opportunity is permanent and cannot be undone.

Deleting a company

  1. Go to Companies and open the company record.
  2. Click Delete Company.
  3. Confirm the deletion in the dialog that appears.
ℹ️
Two things to know before deleting a company
1. A company cannot be deleted if it has active opportunities linked to it. You must delete those opportunities first.
2. Deleting a company also deletes all its contacts permanently.

⭐Tips & Best Practices

A few habits that will make SETU work better for you and your team.

🏢
Company first, opportunity second You cannot create an opportunity without a company to link it to. If the company is not in SETU yet, add it before starting the opportunity form.
📝
Use drafts freely If you don't have all the details yet, save as a draft. It stays private to you until you publish — there is no risk of sharing incomplete information with your team.
🔑
Use the setu-ID when discussing deals The setu-xxx code is the unique reference for every opportunity. Share it with a colleague instead of describing the deal by name to avoid any confusion — especially when multiple deals involve the same company.
💰
Keep deal values accurate If the scope of a deal changes, update the Value field. Your Pipeline page shows the total value per stage — keeping values current gives you and your manager an accurate picture of the pipeline.
📦
Choose BU line items carefully Whichever Business Units you add as line items will determine which BU Heads and BU Managers gain visibility into that opportunity. Make sure the BU selections reflect the actual scope of the deal.
✅
Double-check before closing a deal Once you mark an opportunity as Won or Lost, the record is locked and cannot be edited. Take a moment to review all the closing details before hitting save.
🔗
Share cross-territory deals If a colleague needs to see a deal that's outside their territory or role scope, use the Share button inside the opportunity to add them as a POC. It only gives them access to that one deal.
📅
Use quarter filters for reporting On both the Opportunities and Concluded pages, the Q1–Q4 preset buttons (April–March fiscal year) let you instantly filter to a specific quarter. Useful for end-of-quarter reviews.
